## Original cache side effects

Original `02048260` writes halfwords to the 512-byte buffer `020FB6E8`; original `020482B4` uses `020FB4E8`. They calculate unsigned destination+length modulo 32 bits, reduce length to 512-destination when that sum exceeds 512, then use signed length comparisons and division by two. Destination is rounded down to an even byte offset through its unsigned right shift by one. The modeled source reads are complete halfwords, not bytes or widened words.
